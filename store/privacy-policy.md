# Privacy policy: Editor Themes for Google Workspace

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This add-on doesn't collect, store or share personal data. There are no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.

## What stays on your device

Your settings stay in your browser's own storage for the add-on (IndexedDB) and never leave your device:
- the themes you imported;
- your theme choices and per-theme options;
- the paragraph-style colors the add-on noticed in your documents.

You can export them as a backup file, and removing the add-on removes them.

## Network requests

The add-on makes only these requests, all read-only:
- **Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Drawings and Drive pages you open.** It reads stylesheets and images there (including template and file thumbnails) so it can recolor them. In Google Docs it reads the document's HTML export, which tells it which paragraph style (Title, Heading 1, …) each piece of text uses. These requests go to Google's own servers, just as your browser would make them. Nothing is sent to any other server.
- **GitHub**, only when you choose to import a color scheme: it downloads the scheme file you picked.

## What it never does

- It never writes to your Google account: no edits, copies, comments, document settings or Drive files.
- It never sends your documents, their content, or information about your browsing to the developer or anyone else.
